[SoftGrove Games](https://www.softgrovegames.com/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6765679975) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.petalpop.game)

Petalpop is a cluster-pop puzzle game that I took from concept to release under SoftGrove Games. I owned the product, gameplay, interface, store delivery, and the operational work required to maintain the game on iOS and Android.

## Product scope

- Campaign, daily, endless, and zen play modes
- Special blooms, boosters, combo systems, and progression rewards
- Leaderboards and repeatable daily content
- A calm visual identity carried across the game, store pages, and supporting website

## Engineering and delivery

The project included more than the core puzzle mechanic. I built the progression and content systems around it, integrated in-app purchases and store services, handled consent and monetization flows, and prepared the release assets and metadata for both stores.

Post-launch work includes release management, compatibility updates, store review requirements, player support, and product iteration based on how the complete experience behaves in production.

## What it demonstrates

Petalpop is an example of owning a mobile product end to end: product decisions, software engineering, user experience, publishing, and ongoing delivery rather than a single isolated feature.
