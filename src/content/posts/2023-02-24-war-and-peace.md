<https://warandpeace.com/>

War and Peace is a long-running strategy game with a web platform closely connected to its mobile product. My work has covered its webshop, payment and routing systems, limited-time events, analytics, and ongoing product development.

**Commerce and product flows**

- Migrated payment flows to shared group infrastructure
- Reworked checkout, routing, error handling, and analytics around purchase journeys
- Built event storefronts and limited-time promotional experiences connected to game state
- Implemented A/B tests and product instrumentation for iterative improvements

The platform is built with Svelte and integrates with several backend and payment services. Because commerce changes have a direct operational impact, the work requires careful rollout, observability, and close collaboration with backend, product, and art.
